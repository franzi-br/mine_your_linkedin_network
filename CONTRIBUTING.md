# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve the kit. The most useful contributions are:

1. **Results from your own run.** Open an issue with your counts (connections, matches, messages sent, replies, conversations) and your function and level. Please remove all names of people and companies.
2. **Prompt fixes.** If Claude misread a file, missed a signal, or the LinkedIn export format changed, open an issue with what you saw and, if you can, a pull request against `prompt/prompt.md`.
3. **Playbook practices.** Something that worked or failed in your outreach. Tag where it comes from, as the playbook does.

Never attach or paste your LinkedIn export, your output sheet, or anyone's contact details in an issue or pull request.

Keep changes small and focused: one fix per pull request, with a line in `CHANGELOG.md`.
