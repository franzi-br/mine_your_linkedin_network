# Filled prompt example

A fictional engineering leader, to show the level of detail that works. Names and companies are invented.

```
GOAL
I'm a Senior Engineering Manager at Northwind Software, based in Berlin. I will not relocate.
I'm pursuing two career tracks:
Track A: Head of Engineering at a venture backed scale up, roughly 250 to 700 people.
Track B: Director of Engineering at a large tech company with a Berlin office, roughly 750+ people.
Within 6 weeks I want 10 to 15 advice conversations with people who hire for, or can refer me into, these roles. Outreach asks for advice, not a job.

WHO I'M LOOKING FOR
1. Decision makers: CTO, VP Engineering, founders and COOs at Track A companies; VP Engineering and Senior Directors at Track B companies.
2. Referral sources: Engineering Directors and Heads of Engineering; former colleagues from Contoso, Fabrikam and Northwind now in senior roles; anyone at a company in my target overlap files.
3. Intermediaries: tech focused executive search firms; VC talent partners; heads of talent at target companies.
4. Investors and board members: VC partners, angels and board members.
5. Warm network: anyone with a strong warmth signal at a tech company, in any function.

(DATA, METHOD, OUTPUT, OPENING LINES, LIMITS and ONGOING unchanged from the template, with the brackets filled:
Google Sheet; cap 4s at 40; warm network capped at 20; opening lines in English and German; informal address by default.)
```

Second pass, sent once the shortlist is back:

```
Here is what only I know about some of the matches. Treat it as true, mark it "(your note)", adjust scores where it changes the picture, and rewrite those opening lines.
Jane Doe: my manager at Contoso, 2019 to 2021, informal.
```
